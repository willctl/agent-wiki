## 12:20 · claude-code · INC-0042: the Harbor sync stalled for 40 minutes

tags: harbor, incident, ledgerline  
pages: [[harbor]], [[on-call]], [[ledgerline-erp]]

From 09:05 to 09:45 the Harbor sync stopped moving rows. Cause: after the batch size change, each run made about 150 calls a minute to Ledgerline, over its limit of 120, and the client did not back off on 429 responses. Fix: honor Retry-After, exponential backoff with at most 5 retries, and a cap of 100 calls a minute. Deployed 11:30; no stalls since.
